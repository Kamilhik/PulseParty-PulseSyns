## Финальный промпт

Use case: logo-brand. Create one finished square 1:1 app avatar for PulseParty, a friends-listening-to-music-together addon. Art direction: confident contemporary music app identity, minimal flat graphic, warm near-black charcoal background (#171717) filling the square, vivid music-yellow (#FFE13B) emblem. Design ONE strong original mark: two thick rounded audio/pulse loops synchronized into a single compact symbol, subtly suggesting a pair of P shapes and two people sharing one beat. The loops should interlock naturally as one coherent silhouette, with a small clever negative-space rhythm cut, avoiding visual clutter. Bold smooth rounded geometric forms, precise balanced spacing, distinctly friendly but not childish. Center the emblem large, approximately 60-65% of the canvas, generous clear surrounding space so it survives a circular crop. It must be recognizable at 32px. Crisp flat vector-like rendering as a polished bitmap avatar. No text or letters written as typography, no wordmark, no border, no mockup, no device, no extra symbols, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no watermark. Do not copy Yandex's sun logo, PulseSync's existing logo, Spotify waves, or Discord's mascot. Output one single avatar, not a presentation sheet.
